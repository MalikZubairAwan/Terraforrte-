Frequently Asked Questions

How do I clean glossy tiles?

Sweep first, then mop with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner. Dry with a microfiber cloth to avoid streaks, and avoid abrasive powders, bleach or wax, which can dull the shine.

Can I use indoor tiles outdoors?

Only anti-slip, frost and UV resistant tiles, like our Heavy-Duty Outdoor Tile, should be used outside. Glossy indoor tiles can become slippery when wet.

How many extra tiles should I order?

Order about 10% extra for cuts and breakage, and 15% for diagonal or patterned layouts. The calculator on each tile page adds 10% automatically.

Can I get a custom size or design?

Yes. Send your project details through the quote form and we will suggest options that suit your space and budget.

Can wall tiles be used on floors?

Not always, because wall tiles are usually thinner and softer. Check the Usage line on each tile page, or ask us before you buy.
